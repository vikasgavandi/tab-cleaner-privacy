Tab Cleaner

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Your Privacy Matters

Tab Cleaner does not collect, store, transmit, sell, or share your personal information or browsing data. The extension operates locally within your browser and does not send user data to any backend server.

1. Information We Access

Tab Cleaner may access information about your currently open browser tabs, such as tab titles, URLs, domains, and tab identifiers.

This information is accessed only when required to provide the extension's core functionality, including displaying open tabs, identifying duplicate tabs, and allowing users to close unwanted tabs.

2. No User Data Collection

Tab Cleaner does not collect or capture user data.

The extension does not intentionally collect personal information, browsing history, passwords, authentication credentials, form data, website content, files, or other sensitive information.

3. Memory Usage Information

Tab Cleaner may use browser-provided information about memory usage associated with browser tabs where such information is available.

This information is used locally to display memory usage and help users identify tabs that may be consuming significant system resources.

4. Local Processing

Tab Cleaner processes information locally within the user's browser to provide its tab-management features.

Tab information accessed by the extension is not uploaded to, stored on, or processed by a Tab Cleaner backend server.

5. No Backend Data Collection

Tab Cleaner does not operate a backend service that receives, stores, or processes user browsing information.

No tab URLs, tab titles, browsing activity, memory information, or other extension-generated user information is transmitted to our servers.

6. Data Storage

Tab Cleaner does not store user browsing data on external servers.

The extension does not intentionally store website content, passwords, authentication credentials, form data, personal files, or browsing history.

7. Data Sharing

Tab Cleaner does not sell, rent, or share user information with third parties.

Since Tab Cleaner does not collect or transmit user data to a backend, there is no user browsing data provided to third-party services.

8. Website Content

Tab Cleaner does not collect or analyze the contents of websites for advertising, analytics, profiling, or tracking purposes.

Tab metadata such as URLs and titles may be temporarily accessed locally when required to identify duplicate tabs and organize currently open tabs.

9. Third-Party Services

Tab Cleaner does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services to collect browsing activity or user data.

10. Security

Because Tab Cleaner does not transmit user browsing information to a backend server, user tab information remains within the browser environment while the extension performs its functions.

While reasonable measures are taken to maintain the security of the extension, no software can guarantee absolute security.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of Tab Cleaner change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Tab Cleaner, please contact the developer through the contact information provided on the Chrome Web Store listing.
